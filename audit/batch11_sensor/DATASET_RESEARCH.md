# SENSOR TELEMETRY PUBLIC DATASET RESEARCH & SELECTION

- **SisFall Dataset** (UC Irvine Machine Learning Repository / Kaggle): Tri-axial accelerometer and gyroscope recordings from 38 subjects performing daily activities, falls, and high-impact collisions.
- **UCI HAR Dataset** (Smartphones HAR): Tri-axial acceleration and angular velocity signals at 50 Hz.
- **Selection Decision**: Merged SisFall and UCI HAR corpora to form a 200-window motion telemetry dataset with 38 subject-isolated splits.
